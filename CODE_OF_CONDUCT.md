# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making this project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive space for everyone.

Everyone participating in this project is expected to treat others with respect, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Welcoming new contributors
- Giving helpful feedback
- Accepting different opinions and experiences
- Assuming good intent
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insults, personal attacks, or hostile comments
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate comments
- Any behavior that would reasonably make others feel unsafe or unwelcome

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block users who violate this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement actions may be taken when behavior occurs in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other official communication channel.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainer.
